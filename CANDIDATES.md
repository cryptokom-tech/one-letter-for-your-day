# Candidate letters — local judging

Judged against the judge brief's five criteria, in order:
1. Letter stands for a real day-describing word.
2. Word vivid and specific (sharp picture, not vague mood).
3. Funny or surprising (repeat-to-a-friend line).
4. Feels true to a real day (honest report, not performance).
5. Letter fits its word; not the obvious pick where a sharper one exists.
